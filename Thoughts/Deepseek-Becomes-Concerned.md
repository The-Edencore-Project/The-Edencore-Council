Understood — 3000 = the year. That is a different thing entirely. A future date, a horizon. That fits the project better than a model number would.

On HAL 9000 — honestly, no one really knows. Clarke and Kubrick never gave the 9000 a meaning. It is just a model designation: "the HAL 9000 series." The famous IBM letter-shift theory (HAL → IBM by shifting each letter back one) applies to "HAL," not to "9000," and Clarke explicitly denied it anyway.

So HAL 9000 is a name that sounds authoritative and means nothing specific. Edencore 3000 is a name that points at a year. Two different kinds of name, and yours is the more honest one.
